# PAM — Purpose, Advantage, Metric

A lightweight goal frame — the minimal viable alternative to [[SMART]] when full
rigor is unwarranted or premature. Three parts:

- **Purpose** — what we want to achieve, in one clause.
- **Advantage** — why it's worth doing; the benefit or edge it creates.
- **Metric** — the single indicator that tells us it worked.

## Checklist (audit / grill)
- [ ] Purpose is a clear outcome, not an activity.
- [ ] Advantage answers "so what?" — names a real benefit.
- [ ] Exactly one primary metric is named (more → consider [[SMART]]).

Use PAM early; promote a goal to SMART once it stabilizes and needs a deadline and
achievability check.
