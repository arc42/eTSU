## Glossary

- [GLO-001](../wiki/glossary/GLO-001-tour.md) — Tour: a sequence of Shows, contracted and shipped as one unit.
- [GLO-002](../wiki/glossary/GLO-002-show.md) — Show: one stop of a Tour at a single gallery. *exhibition* and *tour stop* are aliases.
- [GLO-003](../wiki/glossary/GLO-003-provenance.md) — Provenance: documented chain of ownership and custody; the evidence behind authenticity.
- [GLO-004](../wiki/glossary/GLO-004-tsu.md) — TSU: the art business — Head Office, galleries and staff.
- [GLO-005](../wiki/glossary/GLO-005-etsu.md) — eTSU: the digital product this wiki specifies; scope unsettled (ISS-004).
- [GLO-006](../wiki/glossary/GLO-006-conservator.md) — Conservator: cleans and conserves borrowed pieces at tour close; *cleaner* / *restorator* are aliases. **agreed**
- [GLO-007](../wiki/glossary/GLO-007-appraiser.md) — Appraiser: states what a piece is worth. *preliminary* — distinct from an authenticator (ISS-012).
- [GLO-008](../wiki/glossary/GLO-008-lender.md) — Lender: lends a piece to a Tour without selling it. *preliminary* — union of museums and private owners.
- [GLO-009](../wiki/glossary/GLO-009-logistics-partner.md) — Logistics partner: collective term for shipper, insurer and conservator. *preliminary* — not one company.
- [GLO-010](../wiki/glossary/GLO-010-aml-screening.md) — AML screening: checks the buyer, not the piece. *preliminary* — bought or built is unsettled.
- [GLO-011](../wiki/glossary/GLO-011-piece.md) — Piece: one work on a Tour, either for sale or on loan, never both. *Nine words for it across the sources.*
- [GLO-012](../wiki/glossary/GLO-012-artist.md) — Artist: creator whose work a Tour presents; a counterparty before a beneficiary.
- [GLO-013](../wiki/glossary/GLO-013-buyer.md) — Buyer: whoever signs an intent to purchase and is then on a clock.
- [GLO-014](../wiki/glossary/GLO-014-museum.md) — Museum: lends pieces and buys them, at a lower floor than anyone else.
- [GLO-015](../wiki/glossary/GLO-015-gallery.md) — Gallery: a TSU venue where a Show happens; the galleries *are* TSU.
- [GLO-016](../wiki/glossary/GLO-016-home-gallery.md) — Home gallery: per-tour role; where a Tour starts, ends and ships from.
- [GLO-017](../wiki/glossary/GLO-017-minimum-sale-price.md) — Minimum sale price: contractual floor per piece, agreed with the artist.
- [GLO-018](../wiki/glossary/GLO-018-minimum-museum-sale-price.md) — Minimum museum sale price: second, lower floor on the same piece.
- [GLO-019](../wiki/glossary/GLO-019-agreed-price.md) — Agreed price: floats between Shows, fixed within one.
- [GLO-020](../wiki/glossary/GLO-020-commission.md) — Commission: 20–40% to the artist, settled in two halves on two clocks.
- [GLO-021](../wiki/glossary/GLO-021-lending-agreement.md) — Lending agreement: insured value, loan duration, conservation at tour close.
- [GLO-022](../wiki/glossary/GLO-022-intent-to-purchase.md) — Intent to purchase: the signature that starts both payment clocks.
- [GLO-023](../wiki/glossary/GLO-023-the-insult.md) — The Insult: the half-deposit **returned** to a failed buyer, not the half kept.
