# Year-1 targets and the 2029 narrative give different values for the same five metrics

**What's unresolved.** One email carries two number sets for the same five
indicators. Read charitably they are a Year-1 target and a five-year ambition —
but the email never says so, and the objectives can each hold only one
`target:` + `horizon:`. Until the group confirms the reading, it is an
unreconciled contradiction rather than a roadmap.

**Affects.** [[GOAL-002-global-market-expansion]],
[[GOAL-003-operational-excellence]], [[GOAL-004-trust-and-authentication]].

**Context / evidence.**

| Metric | `VISION` block | 2029 newspaper |
|---|---|---|
| Tour setup lead time | 3–4 weeks → 1 week | 3 weeks → 48 hours |
| Provenance coverage | 90%+ | 99% |
| Artist payment | 5 days → real time | 5 days → within 24 h |
| Price premium | 10–15% | 12–15% |
| Digital share of revenue | 15–20% by Year 1 | 48% |

Note the **baseline** also drifts: tour setup starts at "3–4 weeks" in one half
and "three weeks" in the other. And "real time" versus "within 24 hours" is not
a stretch of the same target — they are qualitatively different commitments.

Per the ingest decision, the `VISION` numbers were taken as `target:` at
`horizon: Year 1`, and the 2029 figures recorded in each objective body as a
labelled five-year ambition.

**Options.**
1. Confirm the two-horizon reading, and split each objective into a Year-1 and a
   2029 goal once the interim is met. Clean, but doubles the goal tree.
2. Confirm the two-horizon reading and keep the 2029 figures as narrative only
   (current state). Lightest.
3. Treat the 2029 narrative as a workshop imagination exercise carrying no
   commitment, and strike the figures. Honest about what a future-backwards
   exercise is for.

**Resolution.** *Open.*
