# Six workshop stakeholder entries have no identifiable relationship to eTSU

**What's unresolved.** The Req4Arc Linz list names 18 groups. Twelve map onto a
documented interaction with the tour business and became STK pages. Six do not,
and no source describes what they would do with eTSU. Writing empty personas for
them would pad the wiki; dropping them silently would discard workshop output.

**Affects.** `wiki/stakeholders/` completeness; the Context page's user-roles
section once it exists.

**Context / evidence.** Not written as pages:

| Entry | Why it was held back |
|---|---|
| politicians | No source mentions any political interaction. |
| guards | Gallery security; no eTSU touchpoint described. |
| caterers | The only hook in any source is the champagne at a sale. |
| visitors | Plausible — gallery visitors could be an audience for publicity or an online catalogue — but no source says what they'd do. Becomes real under Reading B of [[ISS-004-scope-inflation-marketplace-vs-back-office]]. |
| media / influencers / photographers | The brief mentions "local publicity" per show; nobody has said whether eTSU supports it. |
| marketing | Same publicity question, from the internal side. |

These are plausibly stakeholders of the **business**; the question is whether
they are stakeholders of the **system**.

**Options.**
1. Ask the group for one sentence per entry: what does this role need from eTSU?
   Anything that survives gets a page; the rest is dropped. Recommended.
2. Write all six as stubs now and let them fill in later — risks six permanently
   empty personas.
3. Drop all six — loses "visitors" and "marketing", which become substantive if
   [[ISS-004-scope-inflation-marketplace-vs-back-office]] resolves toward a
   public marketplace.

**Resolution.** *Open.*
