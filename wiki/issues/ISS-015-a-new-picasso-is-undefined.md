# "A new Picasso" is undefined and unmeasurable in the candidate vision

**What's unresolved.** The candidate vision from the Req4Arc Linz workshop reads:
*"If there is a new Picasso we will have shown and sold it over our Platform."*
**Pablo Picasso** (1881–1973) is the most famous artist of the twentieth century,
so the phrase is understood by everyone in the room as "an artist who turns out
to be historically significant". It is never defined, and it cannot be evaluated.

**Affects.** [[ISS-001-competing-vision-statements]] — this is a property of one
of the two candidates and should be weighed when that Issue is decided.
[[GOAL-001-eTSU-vision]] if the line is adopted.

**Context / evidence.** Three distinct problems with the phrase as a requirement:

1. **No criterion.** What makes someone "a new Picasso" — auction records,
   museum acquisitions, critical reception, longevity? Picasso's own
   significance took decades to settle. A vision nobody can evaluate cannot be
   argued with, which is why it feels so agreeable.
2. **Unfalsifiable in practice.** The claim is conditional ("*if* there is one")
   and retrospective. There is no date at which anyone could say it was met or
   missed.
3. **It contradicts the business as described.** TSU's stated business is
   **young, up-and-coming artists** — the brief is explicit that the founder
   "scours the globe looking for the up-and-comings". Picasso is the endpoint of
   a career, not its start. Read literally the line promises TSU will have shown
   an artist *before* they became significant, which is actually a sharper and
   more testable claim than the phrase suggests.

Note this is *not* an argument against the line. Reading 3 is arguably the best
vision statement anyone has produced for eTSU — it says TSU catches artists early
and the platform is where that happens. It just needs saying in words that carry
that meaning.

**Options.**
1. **Keep the line as the vision, and gloss it.** Add one sentence beneath it
   making Reading 3 explicit — the vision stays memorable, the meaning stops
   being folklore. Recommended.
2. **Rewrite it without the name** — e.g. "The artists who matter in twenty
   years will have had their first tour with us." Same claim, no cultural
   dependency, still not a metric.
3. **Attach a proxy metric** to the objective level rather than the vision:
   share of TSU artists who go on to a museum acquisition or a secondary-market
   sale within N years. Testable, but slow — the signal takes years to arrive.
4. Treat it as decoration and keep the Moore-format text as the vision.

**Resolution.** *Open.* Decide together with
[[ISS-001-competing-vision-statements]].
