# "cleaners / restorer" fuses two unrelated roles

**What's unresolved.** The workshop list has one entry, "cleaners / restorer".
These are two different people doing two different jobs, and only one of them
appears in any source.

**Affects.** [[STK-009-conservator]].

**Context / evidence.** The brief names a **professional cleaner/restorer** who
cleans borrowed pieces at the end of a tour — a *conservator*, engaged under a
contractual obligation written into the lending agreement, alongside the
insurance amount and loan duration. That role became [[STK-009-conservator]].

A **cleaner** in the janitorial sense — someone who cleans the gallery — has no
source and no described eTSU interaction; it belongs with the entries in
[[ISS-005-ungrounded-stakeholder-entries]].

The ambiguity is worth fixing in the glossary as well as the stakeholder list:
"cleaning" in this business means *conservation*, not housekeeping, and a reader
who gets that backwards will misread the lending agreement terms.

**Options.**
1. Confirm only the conservator is meant; drop the janitorial reading. Recommended.
2. Both are stakeholders; split into two pages once someone describes what
   gallery cleaning staff would do with eTSU.

**Resolution.** **Resolved 2026-09-09** by [[STK-001-gus-renoir]], along option 1
and then some. There is one role, and it is the **conservator**
([[GLO-006-conservator]]). *Cleaner* and *restorator* are aliases for it;
*restorer* was a typo for *restorator* and is kept as a third alias so earlier
references still resolve. The janitorial reading is dropped — no such stakeholder
exists. [[STK-009-conservator]] was retitled accordingly and the term added to
the glossary.
