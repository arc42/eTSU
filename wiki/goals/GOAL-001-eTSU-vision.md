# Digital tour and gallery management for Gus Renoir

## Vision  — when `stereotype: vision`   > follows the Geoffrey Moore format (contrast clause optional)

For **gallery tour operators** who **coordinate multi-stop art tours across
artists, owners, museums, shippers, insurers, and buyers without losing track
of who agreed to what**, **eTSU** is a **digital tour and gallery management
platform** that **gives real-time visibility into every piece's status — on
loan, for sale, sold, or shipped — across the whole tour, and keeps
commissions, deposits, and payment deadlines straight**.
