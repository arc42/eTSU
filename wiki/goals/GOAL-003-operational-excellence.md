# Operational excellence in tour setup and settlement

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]]; promotes to [[SMART]] once `horizon` is set

- **Purpose.** Turn the manual, paper-driven tour workflow — artist agreement,
  lending agreements, one shipper and one insurer per [[GLO-001-tour|Tour]],
  per-gallery customs and publicity — into digital processes that run without
  someone holding the whole tour in their head.
- **Advantage.** Halves operating cost per tour, shortens the artist commission
  cycle from five days after tour close to near-immediate, and lets the business
  take on more tours without hiring proportionally more back-office staff.
- **Metric.** Tour setup lead time — see `metric:` / `baseline:` / `target:` / `horizon:`.

**Secondary indicators.** Cost per tour $8–12K → $3–4K; buyer checkout
completion ≥ 85%; artist commission paid in real time rather than 5 days after
tour close. Recorded here because an objective carries a single indicator.

**Five-year ambition** (September 2029 narrative): tour setup in **48 hours**,
artist commission paid **within 24 hours of sale**, gallery staff redeployed
from administration to artist relationships and curation.

**Parent.** [[GOAL-001-eTSU-vision]].
