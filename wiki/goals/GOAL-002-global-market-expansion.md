# Global market reach for TSU artists

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]]; promotes to [[SMART]] once `horizon` is set

- **Purpose.** Break the geographic constraints of the physical tour. Make TSU
  art visible and buyable 24/7 worldwide, rather than only to whoever walks into
  a gallery during the days that tour stop is running.
- **Advantage.** Unlocks markets the tour circuit never reaches (Asia, the
  Middle East), raises sales volume for [[STK-002-artist|artists]], and lowers
  the cost of acquiring each new [[STK-004-buyer|buyer]].
- **Metric.** Share of total revenue earned through digital channels — see
  `metric:` / `baseline:` / `target:` / `horizon:`.

**Secondary indicator.** 5,000+ online buyers within Year 1. Recorded here
rather than in `metric:` because an objective carries a single indicator; see
[[ISS-002-unsourced-objective-baselines]].

**Five-year ambition** (from the September 2029 future-backwards narrative in
[[SRC-001-ulrich-stuerzlinger-goals-and-newspaper]]): 150+ countries served,
digital channels at 48% of total revenue, artists earning 2.5× the legacy tour
average. These are *not* the committed Year-1 targets above —
[[ISS-003-year-one-vs-2029-target-divergence]] asks which set the group commits to.
