# Register as an artist

## Story

As [[STK-001-gus-renoir]] **the owner of [[GLO-005-etsu|eTSU]]** I want **an
online portal for artists**, so that **their registration, contacts and
interests come from them rather than from staff transcription**.

**Acceptance criteria.** *None given.* Nothing says who may register, whether
registration is open or by invitation, or what happens to an artist who registers
and is never toured.

**The card was corrected as it was written.** It first read "As the **gallery
owner**"; *gallery* is struck out and *the owner of eTSU* written in. The
correction distinguishes the owner of the **product** from the owner of the
**business**. This wiki treats both as [[STK-001-gus-renoir]] because no source
describes a separate product owner, **which is the agent's reading of a
correction somebody made deliberately.**
