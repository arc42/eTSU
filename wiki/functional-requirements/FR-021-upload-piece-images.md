# Upload piece images

## Story

As [[STK-001-gus-renoir]] **Head Office** I want **to attach images to a
[[GLO-011-piece|piece]]**, so that **the piece can be presented without being
physically present**.

> [!assumption] Benefit borrowed from a neighbouring card
> The green card reads only "Upload images". The benefit above is taken from the
> artist card in [[SRC-010-workshop-example-mapping-stories]] — *"I want my print
> to be digitized so it can be presented online"* — which is the same capability
> asked for from the other side.

**Acceptance criteria.** *None given.* Nothing says how many images, what
resolution, or who may replace them.

**Digitisation is more than a photograph.** The artist card says *digitized*, and
the deprecated [[EIF-006-business-growth-and-content]] node named photography and
3D capture as a supplier service. Whether Head Office uploads a snapshot or
commissions a capture is undecided, and the two cost very different amounts.
