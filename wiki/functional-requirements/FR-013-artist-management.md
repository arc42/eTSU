# Artist Management

## Epic: Summary

For [[STK-001-gus-renoir|Head Office]], **the relationship with each
[[GLO-012-artist|artist]]** — who they are, how to reach them, what they are
interested in, and what has been agreed with them.

**PROBLEM / GOAL**

Artists are found by the Creative Genius and negotiated with by Head Office, and
nothing holds that relationship except email and memory. The workshop asked for
an **online portal for artists** so registration, contacts and interests are
supplied by the artist rather than transcribed by staff.

**Success metric(s).** Inherited from [[GOAL-007-artist-self-service]].

**Scope.** in: artist identity, contact, interests, registration · out: the
[[GLO-011-piece|piece]] record ([[FR-005-piece-management]]), the contract
([[FR-014-contract-management]]) and money ([[FR-007-settlement-and-compliance]]).
