# Customs authority

**Snapshot.** The national authority each [[GLO-002-show|Show]] must satisfy
before pieces can enter or leave. Head Office tells each gallery when to start
on customs paperwork; the gallery does the filing locally. Low interest in eTSU
itself, but high influence: a hold at the border stops a show.

**Goals.** [[PAM]] (light)
- Complete, accurate declarations, with documented ownership and valuation.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Undeclared or misvalued pieces; unclear ownership on borrowed works.

**Interactions.** No [[UC-...]] or [[FR-...]] captured yet.

> [!assumption]
> The customs-paperwork lead time comes from `raw/TSU-brief.md.md`, still
> un-ingested. Whether eTSU integrates with customs systems or merely produces
> documents for humans to file is entirely open.
