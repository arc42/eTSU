# Gallery manager

**Snapshot.** Runs one of the [[GLO-004-tsu|TSU]] galleries and everything that
happens while a [[GLO-002-show|Show]] is in the building: local publicity,
customs paperwork, hanging the pieces, and closing sales. Owns the sale ritual —
the buyer is led to the back office for a glass of champagne while the paperwork
is drawn up and signed.

**Goals.** [[PAM]] (light)
- Know the current price and sale status of every piece in the room without
  phoning Head Office — [[GOAL-005-frictionless-global-art-trade]].
- Spend time on artists and collectors rather than administration;
  [[GOAL-003-operational-excellence]] explicitly targets this redeployment.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Selling a piece that Head Office has already sold, or quoting a price that has
  since been floated.
- Customs and publicity lead times that arrive too late to act on.
- A digital checkout that strips the deliberately genteel sale ritual — the
  champagne is not decoration, it is how this trade closes deals.
