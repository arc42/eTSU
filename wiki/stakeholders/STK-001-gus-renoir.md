# Gus Renoir

**Snapshot.** Runs the business out of the Head Office in New York, showing
the work of up-and-coming artists found by the company's founder (the
"Creative Genius"). Owns the tour concept end-to-end: negotiating artist and
lending agreements, arranging shipping/insurance, and overseeing sales across
every gallery on a tour. Commissioned this digitization effort.

**Goals.** [[PAM]] (light)
- Replace ad-hoc, memory-dependent tour coordination with a system that
  tracks every piece's status (on loan, for sale, sold, shipped) across all
  tour stops.
- Keep commission, deposit, and payment-deadline rules enforced consistently,
  without manual tracking per buyer.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Losing track of which pieces are for sale vs. on loan (from artists,
  private owners, or museums) as a tour moves between galleries.
- Missed deadlines in the buyer payment sequence (10% within 24h/until
  Monday, remaining 90% within 14 days) going uncaught.
- Coordinating one shipper and one insurer across an entire multi-stop tour.
