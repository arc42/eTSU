# Legal and accounting

**Snapshot.** The internal function behind every binding document TSU produces:
artist agreements, lending agreements, buyer intent-to-purchase paperwork, and
the commission settlements that follow a sale. Also the function that owns the
regulatory exposure [[GOAL-004-trust-and-authentication]] talks about — the
"zero compliance incidents" target lands here.

**Goals.** [[PAM]] (light)
- Every sale reconcilable: deposit, balance, commission halves, insurance
  settlements, across multiple jurisdictions and currencies.
- Contract terms enforced by the system rather than remembered by staff.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Money movements the ledger cannot explain — particularly the split of a
  forfeited deposit ("The Insult": half kept, half returned).
- Cross-border tax treatment as tours run through many countries.
