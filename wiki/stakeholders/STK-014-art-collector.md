# Art collector

**Snapshot.** A private acquirer TSU knows by name and works with over years,
across several [[GLO-001-tour|Tours]], rather than once at a single
[[GLO-002-show|Show]]. The distinction from [[STK-004-buyer]] is the
**relationship, not the transaction**: every collector is a buyer at the moment
of purchase, but a buyer becomes a collector only through recurrence and
cultivation. TSU carries the relationship; the buyer role does not.

**Goals.** [[PAM]] (light)
- See what is coming before the general public does — early access to a tour's
  catalogue, and a first look at pieces matching a known interest.
- Build a coherent collection over time, which needs continuity of record across
  tours rather than one purchase at a time.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Being treated as an anonymous transaction after years of purchases.
- Losing a piece to a faster buyer because notice arrived through a public
  channel.
- The same provenance worry as any buyer, weighted heavier because the exposure
  is cumulative — [[GOAL-004-trust-and-authentication]].
