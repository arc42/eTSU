# Minimum museum sale price

**Definition.** A second floor on the same [[GLO-011-piece|piece]], applying
only when the buyer is a [[GLO-014-museum|museum]], and **almost always lower**
than the [[GLO-017-minimum-sale-price|minimum sale price]].

**In context.** This is the clearest proof in the brief that **who the buyer is
changes what they pay**, which makes buyer segmentation a pricing rule rather
than a labelling preference — see
[[ISS-013-three-way-buyer-segmentation]]. The stated reason is the
[[GLO-012-artist|artist's]] own interest: a piece in a museum collection is
worth more to them than the money forgone.

**Distinguish from.** Any notion of a discount. It is a separately negotiated
floor agreed at the same time as the first one, not a reduction applied later.

> [!note] Notes
> `agreed: false` — sourced from the brief. Open: **who qualifies as a museum**
> for this price. No source defines the test, and the answer is worth money on
> every institutional sale.
