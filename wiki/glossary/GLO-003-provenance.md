# Provenance

**Definition.** The documented chain of ownership and custody of a piece, from
its creation to its present holder, used to establish that the piece is
authentic and lawfully sellable.

**In context.** [[GOAL-004-trust-and-authentication]] makes provenance the thing
eTSU competes on: it targets ≥ 90% *verification coverage* — the share of
catalogue pieces whose chain has been checked — and attributes a 10–15% price
premium to verified work. [[STK-008-museum|Museums]] treat it as a precondition
for acquisition, not a nice-to-have.

**Distinguish from.** *Authenticity* — whether the piece is genuinely by the
artist it claims. Provenance is the **evidence**; authenticity is the
**conclusion**. A piece can have a complete provenance record and still be a
forgery if the record itself is fabricated.
