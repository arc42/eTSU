# Lending agreement

**Definition.** The contract under which a [[GLO-008-lender|lender]] puts a
[[GLO-011-piece|piece]] on a [[GLO-001-tour|Tour]] without selling it. It fixes
three things at once: **the insured value**, **the duration of the loan**, and
**the conservation owed at tour close**.

**In context.** Those three terms reach into three different parts of eTSU.
The insured value is what [[STK-006-insurer|the insurer]] covers. The duration
bounds how long the piece may travel, which constrains the tour schedule. The
conservation clause is why a [[GLO-006-conservator|conservator]] is engaged at
all, and it is **per agreement**, not per tour.

Lending agreements are negotiated **after** artist terms and **before** the
formal contract with the artist. If they cannot be reached, the tour is not yet
a "Go".

**Distinguish from.** The formal contract with the artist, which covers pieces
for sale, commission and price floors. Different counterparty, different
contents, different point in the sequence.
