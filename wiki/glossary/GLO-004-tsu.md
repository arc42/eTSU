# TSU

**Definition.** The art business itself: the Head Office in New York, its
network of galleries, and the staff who put [[GLO-001-tour|Tours]] together and
sell the work on them.

**In context.** TSU is the **organization**; it exists today, on paper and in
person, and it is what the digitisation effort is aimed at. Sources use it as a
possessive throughout — "TSU art", "each TSU gallery", "TSU management".

**Distinguish from.** [[GLO-005-etsu|eTSU]] — the digital product being
specified. TSU is the business; eTSU is the system this wiki holds requirements
for. The two are used almost interchangeably in the source emails, which is
exactly why both entries exist.
