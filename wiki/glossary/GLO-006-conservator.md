# Conservator

**Definition.** The professional who cleans and conserves a borrowed piece when a
[[GLO-001-tour|Tour]] closes, under an obligation written into the lending
agreement alongside the insured value and the loan duration.

**In context.** Every borrowed piece returns to its lender through a conservator,
so the role sits on the critical path of tour close-out, not at its margin. The
person is [[STK-009-conservator]].

**Distinguish from.** Nothing — and that is the point. Earlier sources wrote
*cleaner*, *restorer* and *restorator* as if they might be different people. They
are not. All three name this one role and are recorded as aliases; *restorer* is
a typo for *restorator*. In this business **cleaning means conservation, never
housekeeping**, and there is no janitorial stakeholder.
