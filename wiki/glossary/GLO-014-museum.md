# Museum

**Definition.** An institution that holds a permanent collection, and that
appears on **both sides** of TSU's business: it lends
[[GLO-011-piece|pieces]] to a tour, and it buys them at the
[[GLO-018-minimum-museum-sale-price|minimum museum sale price]].

**In context.** The dual role is the whole point of the term. As a
[[GLO-008-lender|lender]] a museum signs a
[[GLO-021-lending-agreement|lending agreement]] and its piece comes home. As a
[[GLO-013-buyer|buyer]] it pays less than anyone else, deliberately, because a
placement in a museum collection is worth more to the
[[GLO-012-artist|artist]] than the difference in price.

**Distinguish from.** A [[GLO-015-gallery|gallery]], which is TSU's own venue
and never owns what hangs in it. And from a private owner, the other kind of
lender, who signs a different agreement.
