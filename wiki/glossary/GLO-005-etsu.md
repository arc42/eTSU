# eTSU

**Definition.** The digital product being specified in this wiki: the system
that supports [[GLO-004-tsu|TSU]]'s tour and gallery business.

**In context.** Every goal in `wiki/goals/` is a goal *for eTSU*. The workshop
emails also call it "our Platform" and "the platform".

**Distinguish from.** [[GLO-004-tsu|TSU]] — the existing business. Statements
like "48% of *our* revenue is digital" are about TSU's revenue flowing *through*
eTSU; the system does not have revenue of its own.

> [!note] Notes
> **What eTSU is scoped to be is genuinely unsettled.** The brief asks for
> "a digital product supporting the gallery's business" — back-office
> digitisation. The workshop emails describe a public, global marketplace with
> its own buyers, checkout and compliance regime. These are different systems
> wearing one name; see
> [[ISS-004-scope-inflation-marketplace-vs-back-office]]. This entry deliberately
> does *not* pick one — it will be tightened once that Issue closes.
