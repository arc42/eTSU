# Gallery

**Definition.** One of TSU's own venues, where a [[GLO-002-show|Show]] takes
place. The galleries **are** [[GLO-004-tsu|TSU]]: they are not partners, not
neighbours and not customers.

**In context.** A gallery is told its dates, when to expect details, when to
start customs paperwork and when to begin local publicity. Each is run by a
[[STK-003-gallery-manager|gallery manager]], whose back office is where an
[[GLO-022-intent-to-purchase|intent to purchase]] is actually signed, over a
glass of champagne. Customs matters here and nowhere else: the paperwork starts
per gallery, ahead of that gallery's show, and a border hold stops the show
([[STK-007-customs-authority]]).

**Distinguish from.** A [[GLO-016-home-gallery|home gallery]], which is a **role
one gallery plays for one tour**, not a different kind of place. And from a
[[GLO-014-museum|museum]], which owns its collection where a gallery does not.
