# Tour

**Definition.** A sequence of [[GLO-002-show|Shows]], usually presenting the
work of one artist (occasionally a group of artists, when no single artist
has enough work for a full show), running from a "home" gallery back to that
same home gallery.

**In context.** The tour is the top-level unit that Head Office plans and
contracts around: it sets the artist agreement, the lending agreements with
owners/museums, and the single shipping/insurance arrangement that covers all
stops. Prices for pieces can be re-set ("floated") as the tour moves from
stop to stop.

**Distinguish from.** [[GLO-002-show|Show]] — a Show is one stop of a Tour at
one gallery; a Tour is the whole multi-stop sequence.
